About Minesweeper

Minesweeper is the classic puzzle game where you reveal cells on a grid, using number clues to deduce where mines are hidden. Our free version features 3 difficulty levels, a timer, flagging, auto-reveal of empty areas, and best time tracking.

Features

  • 3 difficulty levels: Easy (9×9), Medium (16×16), Hard (30×16)
  • First click is always safe
  • Auto-reveal of connected empty cells (flood fill)
  • Right-click or long-press to flag mines
  • Live mine counter and timer
  • Best time tracking for each difficulty
  • Color-coded numbers (1-8)
  • Works on desktop and mobile
  • 100% free, no signup, no download

How Numbers Work

Each revealed number tells you how many mines are in the 8 adjacent cells. Use logic to determine which cells are safe and which contain mines. Flag suspected mines with right-click.
